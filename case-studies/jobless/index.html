My role got impacted. After 5 years, I had nowhere to go, no task to do, no meeting to attend.

I’m an immigrant. My social life was mostly built around work. When the job disappeared, a lot of the structure around my day disappeared with it, and the city suddenly felt too big.

So I built this, to figure out how to live this new life and to grow my AI design skills, taking a problem all the way to a solution using the tools available on the market today. It was an amazing journey that lasted two months, full of learnings and challenges.

While the design case study is still in progress, you can try it live above and read the full story on my LinkedIn.
